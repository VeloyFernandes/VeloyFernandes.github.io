Selected projects

  • Aalborg University2026 – present

    Flowsheet CoderIn development

    An offline application that reads a process flowsheet drawing and generates a working, validated simulation in DWSIM or Aspen Plus. A fine-tuned vision-language model interprets the drawing, and the simulator verifies the result.

    Aim: reduce manual model set-up time so engineers can focus on design decisions for Power-to-X and renewable energy processes.

    • Vision-language models
    • LoRA fine-tuning
    • DWSIM
    • Aspen Plus
    • Python
  • Vestas Aircoil2025 – 2026

    CFD digital twin of V-type dry coolers

    A multi-region OpenFOAM 13 model of V-type dry coolers. The heat exchanger core is represented as a porous medium with fitted heat transfer correlations, and the model is calibrated against sensor data from a controlled test rig.

    Outcome: agreement with manufacturer rating data to within 10%.

    • OpenFOAM 13
    • Conjugate heat transfer
    • Porous media
    • Model calibration
  • Aalborg University2025

    Green methanol from biogas CO₂

    An Aspen Plus model of a Power-to-X plant converting biogas-derived CO₂ and electrolytic hydrogen into methanol, coupled with a Python model of wind and solar supply for a Danish site, a levelised cost of methanol and a life cycle assessment.

    Outcome: the route is technically feasible; renewable intermittency is the main constraint.

    • Aspen Plus
    • pvlib
    • windpowerlib
    • Techno-economic analysis
    • LCA
    Interactive demoSource code
  • Aalborg University2024 – 2025

    Ice accretion on wind turbine airfoils

    A study of how ice accretion degrades blade aerodynamics in cold climates, combining Ansys Fluent CFD with wind-tunnel tests on 3D-printed iced airfoil models to quantify the loss of lift and increase in drag.

    Recognition: Best Abstract & Presentation, CES 2024.

    • Ansys Fluent
    • Wind-tunnel testing
    • Icing
    • Aerodynamics
    Interactive demoSource code

    Buckling of corrugated panels

    Eigenvalue buckling analysis of trapezoidal and round corrugated panels in Ansys, validated against MATLAB and analytical solutions, including a comparison of how boundary conditions affect the critical loads.
