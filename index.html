EarlierMost recent

  • Flowsheet Coder

    Aalborg University, 2026 to now

    An offline application that reads a process flowsheet drawing and turns it into a working, validated simulation in DWSIM or Aspen Plus. A fine-tuned vision-language model interprets the drawing, and the simulator checks the result.

    Goal: less time on manual model setup, more on design decisions for Power-to-X and renewable energy processes.

    →→
    1. add Mixer "MIX-1"
    2. add Reactor "R-1"
    3. add Separator "SEP-1"
    4. connect MIX-1 to R-1 to SEP-1
    5. solve, then check the mass balance

    Point at a unit in the drawing to see the lines it becomes. An illustration of the idea, not the app's real output.

  • CFD digital twin of dry coolers

    Vestas Aircoil, 2025 to 2026

    An OpenFOAM 13 multi-region model of V-type dry coolers. The heat exchanger core is represented as a porous medium with fitted heat transfer correlations, and the model is calibrated against sensor data from a controlled test rig.

    Result: matched manufacturer rating data to within 10%.

    Fluid enters at 45 °C, air at 25 °C. A simplified effectiveness-NTU model with example values, not the CFD results.

  • Green methanol from biogas CO₂

    Aalborg University, 2025

    An Aspen Plus model of a Power-to-X plant that converts biogas-derived CO₂ and electrolytic hydrogen into methanol, with a Python model of the wind and solar supply for a Danish site, a levelised cost of methanol and a life cycle assessment.

    Result: the route is technically feasible, and renewable intermittency is the main challenge. Wind and solar model on GitHub

    Starts at the project's sizing. A stylised clear summer day with steady wind at 40% of rated power; the real model uses hourly NASA POWER weather data.

  • Icing on wind turbine airfoils

    Aalborg University, 2024 to 2025

    A study of how ice accretion degrades blade aerodynamics in cold climates, combining Ansys Fluent CFD with wind-tunnel tests on 3D-printed iced airfoil models to quantify the loss of lift and rise in drag.

    Awarded Best Abstract & Presentation at CES 2024.

    The direction of the effect only: more ice, less lift and more drag. The bars are not measured values.

  • Buckling of corrugated panels

    Swansea University, 2023

    Eigenvalue buckling analysis of trapezoidal and round corrugated panels in Ansys, validated against MATLAB and analytical calculations, with a comparison of how boundary conditions change the critical loads.

    An Euler column as a stand-in: clamping the edges raises the critical load four times. The project ran eigenvalue analysis on corrugated panels in Ansys.
